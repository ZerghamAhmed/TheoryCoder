BasicGame
    SpriteSet
        floor       > Immovable       img=newset/whiteback.png
        wall        > Immovable       img=oryx/wall1.png     autotiling=True
        greenbox    > Passive         img=newset/blockG.png
        yellowbox   > Passive         img=newset/blockY.png
        bluebox     > Passive         img=newset/blockB.png
        avatar      > MovingAvatar    img=oryx/girl1_noback.png

    LevelMapping
        A > avatar floor
        G > greenbox floor
        Y > yellowbox floor
        B > bluebox floor
        . > floor
        W > wall

    InteractionSet
        # avatar “pushes” boxes by making the box bounce forward
        greenbox    avatar    > bounceForward
        yellowbox   avatar    > bounceForward
        bluebox     avatar    > bounceForward

        # solid blocking against walls
        avatar      wall      > stepBack
        greenbox    wall      > stepBack
        yellowbox   wall      > stepBack
        bluebox   wall      > stepBack



        # make boxes block each other
        greenbox    greenbox    > stepBack
        yellowbox   yellowbox   > stepBack
        greenbox    yellowbox   > stepBack
        yellowbox   greenbox    > stepBack

        bluebox   greenbox    > stepBack
        greenbox   bluebox    > stepBack
        bluebox   yellowbox   > stepBack
        yellowbox   bluebox   > stepBack
        bluebox   bluebox   > stepBack




    TerminationSet
        Timeout     limit=2000  win=False
